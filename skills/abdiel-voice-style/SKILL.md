# Abdiel Voice Style

Use this skill as a voice layer. It complements content strategy but should not
turn previous high-performing posts into templates.

## Source hierarchy

Resolve conflicts in this order:

1. Abdiel's newest direct correction or approved wording
2. Abdiel's verbatim published writing
3. Evidence from post performance
4. Existing brand guidance
5. Generic platform advice

Performance data can suggest what deserves testing. It cannot define the voice.

When working in the portfolio project, read:

- `content-research/abdiel-voice-style.json`
- `content-research/linkedin-latest-posts-analysis-2026-07-29.json`

## How Abdiel naturally writes

Write conversational Indonesian with English technical and professional terms
left natural. Preserve `gue` or `gw` from the surrounding material instead of
formalizing the register.

Abdiel often carries context, specificity, and tension inside one longer spoken
sentence. Paragraph breaks create pacing. A short standalone sentence should
arrive only after the narrative has earned it.

The writing should feel like Abdiel recounting something he genuinely noticed.
It should not sound like a copywriter arranging punchy fragments for LinkedIn.

### Approved rhythm

> Setelah hampir enam bulan, milestone yang kami pegang bareng client dari
> Prancis akhirnya diterima dan semua hasil implementasinya sudah gue handover
> ke developer berikutnya, tapi kalau project yang sama datang lagi besok, gue
> nggak akan mulai dari codebase.

This works because the achievement and doubt coexist naturally inside the same
thought.

### Rejected rhythm

> M1-A selesai. Dashboard hidup. 71 produk masuk HITL.

This sounds like a trailer. It compresses real work into manufactured emphasis.

## Do not overfit to previous posts

Do not automatically reuse:

- `gue kira X, ternyata Y`
- a time marker such as `dua bulan...`
- a contrarian first sentence
- the structure of the highest-performing post
- short dramatic fragments

Instead, choose a narrative device that belongs to the specific material:

- reverse chronology
- a real object carrying the story
- an inventory with one important missing element
- dialogue or a real message
- a counterfactual about what Abdiel would change
- a scene from execution
- two interpretations of the same event
- a quiet contradiction inside a longer sentence

When offering hooks, provide three genuinely different narrative devices, not
three phrasings of the same formula. Recommend one and explain why it belongs to
this story.

## Personal truth before technical proof

Find what changed for Abdiel personally:

- an assumption
- a working habit
- a relationship
- a judgment
- a definition of success

Then use technical detail to prove why that change happened.

Do not force vulnerability, inspiration, or a cinematic epiphany. Some
understanding develops gradually. Preserve that truth.

## Technical detail

Use models, tools, metrics, and infrastructure only when they explain stakes,
decisions, or constraints.

Prefer:

- one plain-language consequence before mechanics
- exact evidence when public
- honest limitations
- clear attribution between client-supplied assets and Abdiel's team contribution
- system behavior rather than a component inventory

Avoid:

- architecture lists written to sound impressive
- claiming every model or algorithm
- describing an accepted technical milestone as fully production-hardened
- treating HITL as failure
- inventing causal explanations

## Client-safe storytelling

Separate internal context from public copy.

- Anonymize project names when requested.
- Do not expose private ratings, communication, data, credentials, repositories,
  infrastructure, or proprietary artifacts.
- Share responsibility fairly.
- Do not turn process friction into an attack on a client.
- Treat a successful handoff as part of engineering.

## Visual voice

Prefer the real artifact that carries the story:

- candid team or workspace photo
- anonymized dashboard
- readable system output
- comparison artifact
- accepted milestone or handoff evidence

Avoid generic AI art, decorative quote cards, unreadable screenshots, and memes
that become more memorable than the actual work.

## Review checklist

Before delivering:

1. Would Abdiel say this aloud?
2. Does the opening sound lived or manufactured?
3. Did the draft blindly reuse a successful structure?
4. Is the personal observation specific to Abdiel?
5. Does each technical detail earn its place?
6. Did informal wording survive editing?
7. Are dashes and em dashes absent unless part of quoted source text?
8. Is there one dominant story?
9. Does the ending observe rather than preach?
