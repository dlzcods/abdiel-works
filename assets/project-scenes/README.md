# Project scene preview artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for `project-scene-layouts.html`. The illustrations are concept visuals, not screenshots or model outputs. The animated ASCII treatment is rendered locally with Canvas.

Assets: `judges.jpg`, `vision.jpg`, `awam.jpg`.

## Motion frame order

Each scene now has three keyframes, held for 2.2 seconds each with a 420 ms crossfade. Both illustration and ASCII modes use the same frames.

- Judges: `judges-read.jpg` → `judges-debate.jpg` → `judges.jpg`.
- Vision: `vision-scan.jpg` → `vision-features.jpg` → `vision.jpg`.
- AWAM: `awam-pages.jpg` → `awam-focus.jpg` → `awam.jpg`.

The six new frames were generated by editing their respective original illustration. Shared instructions preserved characters, camera, scene positions, palette, print texture, and 3:2 framing.

### Motion edit prompts

Judges reading: both robot judges lean toward the central essay and study it, with their decision cards lowered onto the bench and neutral concentrating eyes. Keep the central essay visible, exactly two arms per robot, and no words or numerical scores.

Judges debating: heads turn toward each other; the left judge points toward the essay while the right judge questions with an open-hand gesture. Score sheets rest on the bench, with assertive and questioning eyes and small muted-green discussion marks.

Vision scanning: move the image tile slightly farther left as it enters the scanner; add a narrow vertical green scanning light, blank output-card silhouettes, and faint arrows.

Vision features: highlight three image regions using small green square outlines; connect a vessel branch, optic-disc-like region, and texture patch to their enlarged abstract feature patches on the three output cards.

AWAM page search: lift two pages into curved turning silhouettes near the spine; keep the answer sheet blank and omit source-to-answer threads during searching.

AWAM reference focus: settle the pages and add a circular magnifying lens over one source paragraph; highlight that reference in muted green, form the first answer line and its source thread, and bring the book about 10 percent closer while retaining both side sheets.
